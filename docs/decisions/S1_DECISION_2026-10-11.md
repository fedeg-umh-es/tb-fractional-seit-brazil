## 1. What is decided

The model's monthly observable is redefined as the ordinary monthly integral of the exposed-to-infectious transition,

\[
\widehat y_k=\int_{t_k}^{t_{k+1}}\sigma E(s)\,ds,
\]

evaluated by composite trapezoidal quadrature on the solver states, for both the fractional and the integer model. The previous observable, the increment of a Caputo auxiliary state \(C\) with \({}^CD_t^\alpha C=\tau_0^{1-\alpha}\sigma E\), is retired as the basis for scientific claims. It coincides with the ordinary flow only at \(\alpha=1\).

The evidence of record for the manuscript becomes the S1 freeze listed above. `results_canonical/` and `outputs/` on `main` are retained unchanged as **historical evidence for the retired operator**. They are not overwritten, deleted or reinterpreted, and they are not cited as current results.

## 4. Results that do not change

- The fractional model has lower observed RMSE and MAE than the independently refitted integer model at every horizon h = 1..12.
- The fractional model has negative observed skill against persistence and against SARIMA at every horizon h = 1..12.
- Integer-model, persistence, seasonal-naive and SARIMA predictions are identical under both operators. For the integer model this follows from the two operators coinciding at alpha = 1; the baselines do not depend on the operator.
- beta, gamma and d remain weakly identifiable individually.

The answer to the dominant scientific question is therefore the same under both operators. The changes concentrate on alpha, d, the reference R0 and the seasonal-naive horizon.

## 5. Interpretation limits carried into the manuscript

- alpha and sigma lie at or within 0.004 of their lower bounds. Their fitted values are not interpreted as estimates of biological quantities, and alpha < 1 is not interpreted as evidence of biological memory.
- The R0 range is a practical-identifiability envelope over near-equivalent solutions, not a confidence interval, and refers to the autonomous reference system with frozen demography.
- A stable disease-free equilibrium of the reference system does not imply tuberculosis elimination and does not describe the mechanism generating the fitted trajectory.
- The historical within-family difference is not attributed exclusively to the change of operator.
- All forecast comparisons remain descriptive: 13 origins, overlapping multi-step errors, evaluation window 2021–2022.
